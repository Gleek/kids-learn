Changes: extract the last two pronounced items using detected pauses, convert to mono MP3 at 96 kbps, and remove embedded metadata. Extraction timestamps are in `clips.json`.

The source's documented sequence ends with क्ष and ज्ञ. These filenames are tentative identifications from that sequence and the last pauses. Unlike the UT recordings, the pauses are irregular and contain background noise. Both clips decode, but listening review is required to confirm identities, pronunciation, and boundaries. They are not enabled in the game yet.

No reusable त्र recording has been verified. Do not synthesize it by joining त and र recordings, which would give two separate syllables instead of the conjunct.
