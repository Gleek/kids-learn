**English Reading***
- **Word-to-picture CVC**: show `cat`, `dog`, `sun`; child picks matching image.
- **Picture-to-word CVC**: show image, choose among 3 words.
- **Build the Word**: image shown, child arranges letters: `c-a-t`.
- **Missing Sound**: `c _ t`, pick `a`.
- **Sentence Match**: “The cat is on the mat.” Pick correct scene.
- **Silly Sentence Detector**: “The sun is in the cup.” Child says yes/no.
- **Sight Word Hunt**: tap all instances of `the`, `is`, `my` inside short sentences.
- **Listen and Choose**: parent/audio says “I see a red bus”; child picks picture.

**English Speaking / Comprehension**
- **Answer the Question**: show scene, ask “What is he doing?” with 2-3 answer choices.
- **Why / How Cards**: “Why is the boy wearing a coat?” choose “Because it is cold.”
- **Finish the Sentence**: “I want ___”, “I see ___”, “Can you ___?”
- **Emotion Match**: show face/scenario, choose “happy/sad/angry/scared/tired.”
- **Retell Order**: arrange 3 pictures into beginning, middle, end.

**Math / IQ-Style Reasoning**
- **Pattern Match**: already added. Extend to AB, AAB, ABB, ABC, number patterns.
- **Odd One Out**: three fruits and one vehicle; pick the different one.
- **Which Comes Next?**: visual sequences: seed → sprout → plant → ?
- **Same / Different**: choose which two pictures match by shape, color, size, or count.
- **Matrix Lite**: 2x2 grid: red circle, blue circle, red square, ? = blue square.
- **Complete the Pair**: sock goes with shoe, spoon goes with bowl, rain goes with umbrella.
- **Shadow Match**: match object to silhouette.
- **Size Order**: arrange small, medium, large.
- **Which Has More?**: compare two groups without exact counting when possible.
- **Subitizing Dots**: flash dice/ten-frame dots briefly; choose number.
- **Number Bonds**: show 5 apples, 2 visible and 3 hidden; choose missing number.
- **Story Problems**: “2 birds sat, 1 more came. How many?”

**Shapes / Visual-Spatial**
- **Shape Builder**: already exists. Extend to mosque, house, boat, train, tree.
- **Shape Hunt**: show a room/scene, tap all circles/rectangles.
- **Tangram Lite**: drag 2-4 shapes into an outline.
- **Rotated Shape Match**: match same shape even when turned.
- **Part-to-Whole**: choose which pieces make the shown object.
- **Mirror Match**: choose the mirror image or complete symmetrical butterfly.

**Hindi Reading**
- **Akshar Match**: letter to sound/picture.
- **Find the Letter**: “Find क” among several letters.
- **Letter Families**: group similar-looking letters carefully: ब/व, म/भ etc later.
- **Build Matra-Free Words**: only after varnamala is stable: कम, जल, घर.
- **Picture to Hindi Letter**: कमल → क.

**Arabic / Quranic Reading**
- **Letter Recognition**: pick ب from similar letters.
- **Sound Match**: hear “ba”, choose بَ.
- **Harakat Sort**: match fathah/kasrah/dammah sound.
- **Beginning Sound**: hear/read a Quranic word, identify first letter.
- **Known Surah Words**: match simple words from Al-Fatiha/Ikhlas to audio.
- **Letter Forms Later**: beginning/middle/end forms only when ready.

**Quran / Islamic Foundations**
- **Dua Routine Match**: before eating → Bismillah; after eating → Alhamdulillah.
- **Surah Sequence**: arrange short ayah/audio chunks in order.
- **Prophet Story Sequence**: 3-4 picture cards from Nuh/Yunus/Ibrahim stories.
- **Moral Choice**: scenario appears, choose honest/kind/amanah action.
- **Adab Sorting**: masjid/home/table manners: good choice vs not good choice.
- **Allah’s Creation Sort**: animals, plants, sky, body parts with “Allah made…” framing.

**World Around Us**
- **Animal Habitat Match**: fish → water, camel → desert, bird → nest/tree.
- **Food Source Match**: apple → tree, milk → cow, egg → hen.
- **Weather Dress-Up**: rainy/sunny/cold; choose clothes.
- **Float or Sink Guess**: predict, then parent can test physically.
- **Plant Needs**: choose water, sunlight, soil.
- **Day/Night Sort**: activities or objects into day/night.

**Practical Life / Character**
- **Routine Order**: brush teeth sequence, wudu-like washing sequence, bedtime routine.
- **Clean-Up Sort**: toys to box, clothes to basket, plate to sink.
- **Amanah Game**: “This book is torn. What now?” choose repair/tell parent.
- **Kindness Scenario**: younger child wants toy; choose share/take turns/help.
- **Safety Choices**: road, water, fire, sharp objects.
